# Bulalakaw Station: glossary

**Commander**: the player. Walks the base by day, sits at the Camera Desk by night. Never in danger.
**Crew**: Lani, Maya, Mateo, Carlo. They move between rooms on their own; the Commander watches and directs them.
**Day (build time)**: the untimed part between nights, on the base map. The Commander must go inside every unlocked building before the night can start.
**Building lesson**: the NASA fact, chart and photo shown inside a building on a given day. Each building has a run of lessons, one per day.
**Night**: the timed part (18:00 to 06:00) at the Camera Desk. Won by reaching 06:00 with no lose condition.
**Camera Desk**: the night screen: 2 monitors, 6 cameras (max 2 on), switches, battery forecast.
**Problem**: something broken in one room that only shows on that room's camera (loose cable, clogged filter, leak, cracked seal). Tapping it opens its Task; finishing the Task fixes it.
**Crew request**: a crewmate asks the Commander a yes/no question; answered by opening their camera and tapping them. Each answer has a cost.
**Discovery**: the one science find hidden on a camera each night; tapping it logs it.
**Sponsor deal**: an optional offer from a made-up company before a night: materials if you survive, but sponsor messages cover a monitor during the night.
**Stars**: three per night, the same three every night. **Survival star**: reach 06:00. **Science star**: solve the Malfunction AND find the Discovery. **Challenge star**: the night's own goal (e.g. battery above 20% at sunrise).
**Task**: a short hands-on repair or job (10–20 s) opened from a camera; ends with a one-line "why this works".
**Malfunction**: the night's one hidden maintenance fault with a few possible causes. Never a suspicious crewmate: only a broken part or system.
**Clue**: evidence about the Malfunction seen on a camera; it makes some causes more likely and others less. A clue can come from a sensor that is only usually right (e.g. "right 4 times out of 5"), so one clue is not proof.
**Likelihood**: how probable each possible cause of the Malfunction is right now, shown as a bar per cause.
**Diagnosis**: the Commander's pick of the Malfunction's cause. Right: fixed, counts toward the Science star. Wrong: costs battery and crosses that cause off; try again.
_Rule_: failing a Task or a Diagnosis never loses the night by itself; it only costs battery, air or time.
**Story beat**: one night's piece of the storyline. It decides that night's Malfunction and Tasks.
**Radio fact**: a short real fact from Mission Control, timed to tonight's events.
**Scan**: a per-camera button showing a quick fact card about that room right now. Costs a little battery. Reveals that room's Clue if there is one; "nothing unusual" is also evidence and lowers the causes tied to that room.
**Malfunction board**: replaces the hanging scroll at night: one Likelihood bar per cause and the DIAGNOSE button. The three Stars sit in a strip under the clock.
_Avoid_: "impostor", "sus", "vote against a crewmate". The crew are never the cause.
**Materials**: the only currency; earned by winning nights, spent on building upgrades.
**Supply ship**: the campaign goal: keep the crew safe for 7 nights until it lands.
