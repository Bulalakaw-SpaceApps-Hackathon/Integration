# Bulalakaw Station — Complete Data & Fact Inventory

> **Purpose:** Master inventory of **every** piece of data, scientific fact, and gameplay number used in **Bulalakaw Station**—whether from NASA, general science, or game balance—clearly labeled with its exact file location, in-game usage, basis/origin, and source link.  
> **Live Google Sheet (3 Tabs — Option A):** [Bulalakaw Station Data Inventory](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1z56XgJ8wILUlcb05HIBvTid3LWivkAfwYFvGSoLTu8Q/edit)  
> - **`Overview`**: High-level 16-row map (`D-01`–`D-16`) across all game systems (including summarized Game Balance).  
> - **`NASA_Photos`**: All **39 individual NASA photos** (`P-01`–`P-39`) with exact `https://images.nasa.gov/details/...` URLs, asset IDs, captions, and swap-candidate notes.  
> - **`Facts_and_Claims`**: All **110 individual factual statements & telemetry claims** (`F-001`–`F-110`) across Plot Lessons, Nightly Discoveries, Night Events/Hazards, Codex Entries, Radio Lines, and Repair Task Facts, each with its `Basis / Origin`, source URL, and verification status.  
> *(Cross-referenced with `docs/FACT_CHECK.md` on `origin/final-gameplay`.)*

## Legend: Data Basis / Origin
* **`NASA Official (Live)`** — Live telemetry fetched from `api.nasa.gov` (cached 24 h with offline fallback).
* **`NASA Official (Archived)`** — Real, published NASA mission measurements, dates, or `images.nasa.gov` photography.
* **`NASA + Simplified`** — Grounded in real NASA science, scaled or translated into kid-friendly units (e.g., *chest X-rays*, *5 s per real minute*).
* **`General Science`** — Standard physics/astronomy/biology facts (e.g., speed of light, CO₂/O₂ basics, solar panels needing sunlight).
* **`Game Balance`** — Tuned gameplay numbers (battery drain rates, upgrade costs, 180 s night length) designed to teach trade-offs without frustrating ages 7–12.
