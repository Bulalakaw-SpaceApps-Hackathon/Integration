# Bulalakaw Station: Story Template

Fill in one section per night (Nights 1–7). Keep every line short: kids aged 7–12 read it during play.
The game loads this into `data/story.json`. Placeholder story beats are already in the game, so any night you skip still works.

## Rules for the writer
- **Max two short sentences** per line.
- **The Malfunction is always a broken part or system. A crewmate is never the cause.**
- **Use real Mars and NASA facts only.** If you're unsure, ask the team; don't invent numbers.
- **The true cause must have at least one Clue in its own room.** Rooms are the 6 cameras: SKY RADAR, AIRLOCK, SOLAR FIELD, SHELTER, LIFE SUPPORT, GREENHOUSE.
- **Clue reliability** is "right N in 5 times": 5 means always right. Use 2–3 for a sensor that is often wrong, which teaches that one clue isn't proof.
- **Timing:** a night is 180 seconds (18:00 to 06:00, 15 seconds per game hour). Put clues between 20 s and 160 s, about 30 s apart.
- **Pace:** something should happen every 12–15 s across clues, Tasks, radio facts and crew requests.
